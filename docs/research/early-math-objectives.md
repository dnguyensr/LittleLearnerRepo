# Early Mathematics — Objective Map

> **Version:** 2026-09-07
> **Scope:** Product objectives for pre-K through early first grade. This is a
> design record, not a developmental timetable or a claim of standards
> alignment.

## How to use this map

The objective ids below describe mathematical relationships, not screens or
teaching brands. Presentation code may use objects, dots, frames, number lines,
blocks, stories, or notation, but those are ways to teach an objective rather
than separate objectives themselves.

The sequence is provisional. A prerequisite means the app should normally
model that relationship first; it does not mean every child must complete a
fixed linear course. Parent-pinned activities may remain available for
exploration without contributing readiness evidence.

Each objective follows these shared rules:

- A new relationship receives a short model, a guided turn, and an independent
  turn before it can be marked ready.
- Corrected or guided work is recorded as assisted. It may celebrate but cannot
  establish readiness.
- Immediate app performance is described as **ready in recent practice**, not
  mastery. Confirmation occurs in a later session with different values,
  arrangements, story contexts, or representations.
- Speech, visuals, and touch targets provide redundant access. Speech-off use
  remains operable, but an adult may need to provide mathematical language.
- Optional standards codes are compatibility notes maintained outside child
  presentation. A mapping is not evidence that the activity is effective or
  that the full standard is covered.

declared core until their prerequisite objectives, instructional sequences, and
child-observation gates are documented.

## Source register

P19's target skills retain one bounded readiness item and taught-representation
metadata. A later check changes quantities plus unknown-part position or taught
representation, after a different page session and a 24-hour minimum delay.
The timing and five-of-six threshold are product heuristics; neither is a
validated retention or mastery measure. These new interactions await the P18
child-observation round and physical-device checks.

Runtime `bondTo10` maps to partner-to-ten objective `make-ten`. Runtime `makeTen`
is addition across ten (11–18), a distinct strategy whose guided lesson remains
pending; stored ids are preserved.
